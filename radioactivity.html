α · Alpha

Two protons and two neutrons — identical to a helium-4 nucleus. Emitted by heavy nuclei undergoing alpha decay.

Charge:+2
Mass (u):4
Ionisation:Very high
Penetration:~few cm air, stopped by paper
Speed:~5–7% of c
β · Beta

A fast electron (β⁻) or positron (β⁺) emitted when a neutron transforms into a proton (or vice versa).

Charge:−1 (or +1)
Mass (u):~1/1836
Ionisation:Moderate
Penetration:~1–3 mm aluminium
Speed:up to ~99% of c
γ · Gamma

High-energy electromagnetic radiation emitted when a nucleus moves to a lower energy state after α or β decay.

Charge:0
Mass:0
Ionisation:Low
Penetration:Several cm lead / concrete
Speed:c (speed of light)
Half-life equation

The number of undecayed nuclei N at time t:

N = N₀ × (½)t / t½
After n half-lives: N = N₀ / 2ⁿ. Decay constant λ = ln 2 / t½ ≈ 0.693 / t½.
