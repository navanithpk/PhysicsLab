Galton Board (Bean Machine): Each marble bounces left or right at every peg with probability p. After n rows the landing column follows a Binomial(n, p) distribution — which converges to a Gaussian as n grows (Central Limit Theorem).
Bin(n,k) = C(n,k)·pᵏ·(1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ
μ = np   σ² = np(1−p)
