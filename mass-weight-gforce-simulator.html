Key Distinction
Mass is the amount of matter in an object.
It is measured in kilograms (kg) and never changes.

Weight is the gravitational force acting on mass.
It is measured in Newtons (N) and changes with gravity.
Common misconception: "I weigh 70 kg." — Incorrect! Mass is 70 kg. Weight is ~686.7 N on Earth.
Fun fact: On the Moon your mass stays 70 kg but weight drops to ~114 N — you'd feel like lifting a feather!
